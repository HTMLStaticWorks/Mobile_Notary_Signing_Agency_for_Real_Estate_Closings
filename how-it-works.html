In Detail

What Happens at Each Stage

Step 1. Submit Your Request

Getting started is easy. Title company clients can submit signing requests through our secure dashboard, by phone, or by email. Simply provide:

  • Borrower name, phone, and signing location
  • Preferred date and time window
  • Document package type (purchase, refi, etc.)
  • Any special instructions or requirements
  • Upload documents or provide download link

Step 2. Agent Assignment

Our intelligent matching system pairs your signing with the best available agent based on location, experience, and document type expertise.

  • Agent assigned within 30 minutes
  • Confirmation sent to you and the borrower
  • Agent reviews documents before the appointment
  • Borrower receives appointment reminder
  • Real-time status updates in your dashboard

Step 3. The Signing Session

Our agent arrives prepared and professional, guiding the signer through every page with clarity and patience while ensuring legal compliance.

  • ID verification per state requirements
  • Page-by-page guidance through documents
  • Proper witnessing and notarization
  • Borrower questions answered professionally
  • Signing status updated in real-time

Step 4. Document Delivery

After the signing, your completed package is secured and delivered back to you digitally and physically with speed and care.

  • High-resolution scan uploaded within 2 hours
  • Documents accessible via your dashboard
  • Originals shipped overnight (FedEx/UPS)
  • Tracking number provided immediately
  • Invoice generated automatically
FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can you assign a signing agent?

Most signing requests are confirmed within 30 minutes. For same-day requests in metro areas, we can often assign an agent within 15 minutes of your submission.

Are your agents E&O insured?

Yes, every SignSure signing agent carries a minimum of $100,000 in Errors & Omissions insurance. Many carry up to $1 million in coverage.

What types of documents can you handle?

We handle all standard real estate closing documents including purchase, refinance, HELOC, reverse mortgage, construction loans, modifications, and general notary services like power of attorney and affidavits.

How do I access completed documents?

Completed documents are scanned and uploaded to your secure dashboard within 2 hours of the signing. You'll receive an email notification when they're ready for download.

Do you offer weekend and evening appointments?

Absolutely. We understand closings don't wait for business hours. Our agents are available 7 days a week, including evenings, holidays, and last-minute appointments.
