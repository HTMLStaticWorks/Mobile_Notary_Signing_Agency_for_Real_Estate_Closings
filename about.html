Our Beginning

From a Single Notary to a National Network

SignSure Mobile Notary was born from a simple observation — title companies needed more reliable, professional notary signing agents they could trust with their most important closings.

Founded in 2009 by veteran closing agent Maria Gonzalez, SignSure started as a local signing service in Southern California. Today, we serve title companies, lenders, and settlement agents across 48 states with a network of over 3,000 background-checked and certified notary professionals.

Our commitment remains unchanged: deliver every signing on time, error-free, and with the professionalism your clients expect.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

From humble beginnings to nationwide coverage — here's how we grew.

2009

SignSure Founded

Maria Gonzalez launches SignSure Mobile Notary in Los Angeles, CA with a team of 5 certified signing agents.

2012

West Coast Expansion

Extended operations across California, Oregon, Washington, and Nevada. Reached 500+ completed signings per month.

2015

National Network Formed

Built a vetted network of 1,000+ notary signing agents covering 30 states. Launched the first version of the client dashboard.

2018

Technology-First Approach

Deployed real-time tracking, automated scheduling, and digital document delivery. Reduced turnaround time by 60%.

2021

10,000+ Monthly Signings

Surpassed 10,000 monthly signings milestone. Partnered with 500+ title companies and national lenders.

2026

Industry Leader

Operating in 48 states with 3,000+ agents. Setting the standard for mobile notary signing services in real estate.
