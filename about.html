Our Beginning

From a Single Notary to a National Network

SignSure Mobile Notary was born from a simple observation — title companies needed more reliable, professional notary signing agents they could trust with their most important closings.

Founded in 2009 by veteran closing agent Maria Gonzalez, SignSure started as a local signing service in Southern California. Today, we serve title companies, lenders, and settlement agents across 48 states with a network of over 3,000 background-checked and certified notary professionals.

Our commitment remains unchanged: deliver every signing on time, error-free, and with the professionalism your clients expect.

Zero-Error Standard

Rigorous Quality Assurance & Compliance

In real estate closings, a missed initial or unclear notary stamp can halt funding. We eliminate closing table delays through institutional-grade QA.

  • Double-Audit Document Inspection: Every signing package is checked page-by-page before scan-backs are released.
  • $1,000,000 E&O Insurance: Comprehensive Errors & Omissions insurance protecting lenders, title officers, and buyers.
  • NNA Certified & Background Checked: 100% of our agents undergo annual federal background investigations.
  • 2-Hour Guaranteed Scan-Back: Executed packages delivered digitally within 120 minutes of signature completion.
